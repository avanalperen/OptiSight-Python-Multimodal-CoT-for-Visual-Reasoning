Project : OptiSight : A Python-Driven Multimodal CoT Framework for Visual Reasoning.

“OptiSight: A Python-Driven Multimodal CoT Framework for Visual Reasoning,” will involve the development of an integrated multimodal system capable of combining 
visual perception with structured reasoning. The project leverages a Vision-Language Model (VLM) to 
perform object-level visual recognition and supports higher-order reasoning tasks through Chain-of-
Thought (CoT) methodologies. A key objective of the work is the design of an architecture optimized 
for efficient deployment on specialized hardware, ensuring minimal computational overhead and high 
real-time performance. To this end, the system will be implemented primarily in Python, while Kornia 
will be employed to support differentiable image processing and visual operations within the
framework.
